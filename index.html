Privacy Policy

App Name: CoverMate

Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Overview: CoverMate is built to help students and professionals create assignment and project cover pages easily. We respect your privacy. All your designs, logos, and generated PDF files remain strictly on your personal device and are NEVER uploaded to any private servers.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

Device Storage & Photos (Local Access Only):

  • Photo / Gallery Access: If you choose to pick an institutional or university logo for your cover page, the app accesses your selected image locally. This image is only used in-memory to generate your document.
  • Document Storage: When you export or save a generated cover page PDF, it is saved directly to your device's local storage or shared via your device's native share sheet.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

CoverMate displays advertisements through Google AdMob to support ongoing development and keep the app free. Google AdMob may collect and use certain diagnostic and device data to display relevant advertisements.

These third-party service providers have their own Privacy Policies addressing how they use such information:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543?hl=en

Google AdMob may collect:

  • Anonymous Device IDs and Advertising IDs (e.g., Google Advertising ID).
  • IP address (for coarse geolocation).
  • App performance, crashes, and diagnostics data.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

CoverMate does not directly use cookies. However, third-party libraries such as Google Mobile Ads SDK may use identifiers to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements according to your device settings.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.

5. Security

We value your trust in using our application. Since the app functions primarily locally on your device without an external database, your created documents and templates remain under your control.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates will become effective immediately upon posting to this page.
