LEGAL & PRIVACY COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy

Application: CoverMate: Cover Page Maker

Package ID: com.muhaiminul.covermate

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

Privacy Commitment: CoverMate is designed with strict on-device privacy principles. All academic profiles (names, IDs, batch, departments) and custom university logos remain strictly on your local device storage. We do not operate external database servers to store or sell your academic records.

1. Academic Data & Local Processing

All academic details entered into CoverMate are processed solely on your local smartphone:

  • Profile Information: Student Name, Student ID, Department, Program, Batch, Course Titles, and Teachers' details are saved in your device's private local storage (SharedPreferences). This data is never sent to our servers.
  • Custom Varsity Logos: Any institution logo you pick from your gallery is stored locally in your app's isolated directory so you don't have to re-upload it.
  • Document Generation: All PDF rendering occurs completely offline on your device using vector graphic processing.

2. Third-Party Services

To support ongoing maintenance and provide app reliability, CoverMate integrates verified third-party SDKs provided by Google:

Google AdMob

We display banner and interstitial advertisements to keep CoverMate free for all students. Google AdMob may collect and process anonymous device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), diagnostic metrics, and coarse geographic information to serve advertisements.

Learn more: Google Privacy & Terms

Firebase Remote Config

CoverMate utilizes Firebase Remote Config solely to check the minimum required build number (Force Update mechanism). This ensures all users receive critical security patches and template bug fixes without storing personal identities.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

CoverMate requests only minimal permissions necessary for its intended utility:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Needed to fetch sponsor advertisements and verify app update requirements from Firebase Remote Config.
  • Photo / Gallery Access: Requested only when you intentionally choose to upload a custom university logo from your phone.
  • Storage Access: Used solely to save the final PDF document into your device's Downloads folder.

4. Children's Privacy

CoverMate is designed for college and university students and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your academic data is stored only on your local device, you have complete control over it. You can erase all saved profile information at any time by pressing Reset inside the app or by clearing the app data/cache in your Android system settings.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or legal guidelines. Any revisions will be published directly on this page with an updated date.
